## 项目简介

本研究面向全球城市低空交通发展，构建融合城市形态、地形、人口分布、交通网络与低空基础设施部署的综合评估框架，对 475 个城市的可飞行低空空间与起降资源进行量化，并进一步模拟城市空中交通（UAM）融入现有交通系统后的可达性变化。

研究重点评估不同城市及不同人群获得的出行时间收益与服务公平性改善，揭示低空资源、城市空间结构与交通机会之间的关系，为垂直起降设施布局、低空交通基础设施规划及更公平的城市空中交通发展提供全球尺度的科学依据。

## Overview

This research addresses the development of urban low-altitude transportation worldwide. We construct a comprehensive evaluation framework that integrates urban form, terrain, population distribution, transportation networks, and low-altitude infrastructure deployment, quantifying the flyable low-altitude airspace and vertiport resources of 475 cities, and further simulating accessibility changes after Urban Air Mobility (UAM) is integrated into existing transportation systems.

The study focuses on assessing travel-time benefits and service-equity improvements across different cities and population groups, revealing the relationships among low-altitude resources, urban spatial structure, and transportation opportunities. It provides global-scale scientific evidence for vertiport siting, low-altitude infrastructure planning, and the development of more equitable urban air mobility.
