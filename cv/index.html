CURRICULUM VITAE
Wentong Guo

我目前是浙江大学交通运输工程博士生，导师为金盛教授，并在加州大学伯克利分校开展访问研究。我的研究主要围绕城市交通、GeoAI 和城市计算展开。我比较关注如何利用街景、遥感、车辆轨迹以及其他多源城市数据，更好地理解城市交通和城市空间中的实际问题。目前的研究涉及交通安全、事故风险感知、城市可达性、低空交通、极端热环境以及城市基础设施智能分析等方向，也希望通过多模态学习和时空建模，把人工智能方法真正用于复杂城市系统的理解和优化。I am a Ph.D. student in Transportation Engineering at Zhejiang University, advised by Prof. Sheng Jin, and currently a visiting scholar at UC Berkeley. My research centers on urban transportation, GeoAI, and urban computing. I am particularly interested in leveraging street view imagery, remote sensing, vehicle trajectories, and other multi-source urban data to better understand real-world problems in urban transportation and urban space. My current work covers traffic safety, accident risk perception, urban accessibility, urban air mobility, extreme heat environments, and intelligent analysis of urban infrastructure, aiming to bring AI methods into the understanding and optimization of complex urban systems through multimodal learning and spatiotemporal modeling.

科研经历Research Experience

01
Heat-Resilient 15-Minute Cities: Global Assessment of Walking Accessibility and Thermal Burden
2026.04 – 至今Now
Supervisor: Prof. Sheng Jin and Lu Liang

构建全球尺度的 15 分钟城市热暴露与可达性评估框架，耦合街道级热环境、人口分布与基本服务步行可达性；量化标准化步行路径上的累积热负担与不平等，并评估定向降温策略，以识别极端高温下最需要热干预的城市和街道路段。Developing a global framework to evaluate heat exposure and accessibility within 15-minute cities by coupling street-level thermal environments, population distribution, and walking-based access to essential services. The study quantifies cumulative heat burden and inequality along standardized walking paths and evaluates targeted cooling strategies to identify cities and street segments where thermal interventions could most effectively improve equitable access under extreme heat.

02
Global Urban Air Mobility Accessibility and Service Equity
2025.12 – 2026.04
Supervisor: Prof. Sheng Jin

构建全球尺度框架，评估城市空中交通（UAM）对 475 个城市可达性与交通公平的潜在改善。研究整合城市结构、人口、交通网络与垂直起降点部署情景，量化可达性和不平等变化，并分析 UAM 对服务不足地区出行时间负担及低空基础设施公平规划的作用。Developed a global-scale framework to assess how Urban Air Mobility (UAM) could improve accessibility and transportation equity across 475 cities. By integrating urban structure, population, transport networks, and vertiport deployment scenarios, the study quantified accessibility and inequality changes, showing that UAM could reduce travel-time burdens in underserved areas and support more equitable low-altitude infrastructure planning.

03
Scene-Conditioned Multimodal Learning for Urban Road Crash Risk Prediction
2025.11 – 2026.03
Supervisor: Prof. Sheng Jin

面向约 7,000 条城市道路，融合序列街景影像、逐小时交通流与路网拓扑，构建多模态道路碰撞风险预测框架。通过场景条件特征调制与自适应双图学习捕捉道路环境、交通动态和空间依赖之间的异质交互。Developed a multimodal road crash risk prediction framework for approximately 7,000 urban road segments by integrating sequential street-view imagery, hourly traffic flow, and road-network topology. The framework combines scene-conditioned feature modulation with adaptive dual-graph learning to capture heterogeneous interactions between roadway environments, traffic dynamics, and spatial dependencies.

04
Activity-Based Urban Heat Burden and Spatial Inequality
2026.04 – 2026.07
Supervisor: Prof. Sheng Jin and Lu Liang

通过整合 5 m 热环境与逐小时手机信令活动数据，构建活动–PET 耦合框架，评估 70 个中国城市的潜在热负担。提出互补的集中度与不平等指标，并结合空间分析与可解释机器学习揭示热负担的多尺度驱动因素和定向降温优先区域。Developed an activity–PET coupling framework to assess potential heat burden across 70 Chinese cities by integrating 5-m thermal environments with hourly mobile-signaling-derived activity data. The study proposed complementary concentration and inequality indicators and applied spatial and interpretable machine-learning methods to reveal multiscale determinants of heat burden and identify priority areas for targeted urban cooling.

05
Charging-Station Cold-Start Forecasting with Multimodal Urban Data
2026.06 – 2026.08
Supervisor: Prof. Sheng Jin

面向中国 8 个城市的电动汽车充电需求，融合地理关系、街景与遥感影像以及历史充电负荷，构建归纳式冷启动预测框架。利用概率图重建和不确定性感知时序预测推断新建充电站的潜在需求并提升未来负荷预测。Developed an inductive cold-start forecasting framework for EV charging demand across eight Chinese cities by integrating geographic relationships, street-view and remote-sensing imagery, and historical charging loads. The framework combines probabilistic graph reconstruction with uncertainty-aware temporal forecasting to infer latent demand at newly deployed stations and improve future charging-load prediction.

06
Cross-Scale Explainable Multimodal GeoAI for Urban Traffic Safety
2026.02 – 2026.04
Supervisor: Prof. Sheng Jin

面向 11,249 条道路，融合俯视影像、序列街景与城市功能环境，构建跨尺度多模态 GeoAI 事故热点识别框架；结合异质表征学习、自适应多模态融合与可信度验证的可解释性方法，揭示跨尺度城市环境如何共同塑造道路安全风险。Developed a cross-scale multimodal GeoAI framework for accident-hotspot identification across 11,249 road segments by integrating overhead imagery, sequential street-view imagery, and urban functional context. The framework combines heterogeneous representation learning, adaptive multimodal fusion, and faithfulness-validated explainability to reveal how cross-scale urban environments jointly shape roadway safety risk.

07
Pixel-Level Urban Traffic Risk Mapping with Multimodal GeoAI
2025.04 – 2026.01
Supervisor: Prof. Sheng Jin

开发 CRFPI-Net，融合街景、卫星影像、路网信息与更大尺度遥感上下文进行像素级城市交通风险制图；基于历史碰撞构建细粒度风险标签，并通过异质特征融合、动态特征相关建模和宏观上下文引导推断生成连续风险地图。Developed CRFPI-Net for pixel-level urban traffic risk mapping by integrating street-view imagery, satellite imagery, road-network information, and broader remote-sensing context. The framework constructs fine-grained spatial risk labels from historical crashes and combines heterogeneous feature fusion, dynamic feature-correlation modeling, and macro-context-guided inference to generate continuous urban traffic-risk maps.

Fine-Grained Intersection Risk Mapping through Satellite–Street-View Fusion
2025.04 – 2025.08
Supervisor: Prof. Sheng Jin

融合卫星影像和序列街景，构建细粒度城市交叉口风险制图的多模态深度学习框架；通过宏观/微观尺度特征学习与自适应异质融合捕捉跨模态环境交互，并验证其相较单模态方法的风险感知优势。Developed a multimodal deep-learning framework for fine-grained urban intersection risk mapping by integrating satellite and sequential street-view imagery. The framework combines dedicated macro- and micro-scale feature learning with adaptive heterogeneous fusion to capture cross-modal environmental interactions, demonstrating improved traffic-risk perception over single-modality approaches.

Developing a deep neural network comprising dynamic and static branches based on street view data to identify urban speeding hotspots and frequent speeding factors
2025.02 – 2026.05
Supervisor: Prof. Sheng Jin

根据 PDF 原文：设计两阶段 MaaS 套餐选择陈述偏好调查，并开发双分支动态–静态特征融合网络，以捕捉宏观空间上下文和序列街景中的微观变化；框架结合多尺度上下文聚合、自适应特征融合和基于 CAM 的视觉解释，并通过对比与消融实验验证模型性能。Designed a two-stage stated-preference survey for MaaS bundle choices and developed a dual-branch dynamic–static feature fusion network to capture macro-scale spatial context and micro-scale changes from sequential street-view imagery. The framework integrates multi-scale contextual aggregation, adaptive feature fusion, and CAM-based visual interpretation, with comparative and ablation experiments validating model performance.

Research on constructing a risk perception feature fusion network for pixel level inference of intersection traffic accident risk maps
2023.05 – 2023.12
Supervisor: Prof. Sheng Jin

融合街景、卫星影像、路网和宏观遥感上下文，开发端到端多模态交叉口像素级交通风险制图框架；通过异质特征融合、动态相关建模和宏观上下文引导捕捉互补的多尺度风险模式，并使用对比与消融实验验证有效性。Developed an end-to-end multimodal framework for pixel-level traffic risk mapping at urban intersections by integrating street-view imagery, satellite imagery, road networks, and macro-scale remote-sensing context. The framework combines heterogeneous feature fusion, dynamic correlation modeling, and macro-context guidance to capture complementary multi-scale risk patterns, with comparative and ablation experiments validating its effectiveness.

Research on Constructing a Multi-layer Feature Association Fusion Network for Urban Road Crack Disease Segmentation Method
2024.01 – 2024.05
Supervisor: Prof. Sheng Jin

针对复杂背景与不同工况下的城市道路裂缝分割，构建多层特征关联网络；结合 CNN–Transformer 双分支编码、全局–局部特征关联和轻量级 hybrid-MLP 解码器，以增强多尺度裂缝感知，尤其改善斑马线和低照度干扰场景。Developed a multi-layer feature association network for urban road crack segmentation under complex backgrounds and varying working conditions. The framework integrates CNN–Transformer dual-branch encoding, global–local feature association, and a lightweight hybrid-MLP decoder to improve multi-scale crack perception, particularly under zebra-stripe and low-light interference.
